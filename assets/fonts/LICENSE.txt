These are the real thing: each size is separately drawn, so helvR10 is
not helvR08 scaled. fontbake reads .bdf directly and copies the pixels,
so the SIZE argument is ignored and every bake is 0.0% gray.

Kenney{Mini,High,Pixel,Blocks}.ttf — by Kenney (www.kenney.nl), CC0.
Genuine pixel-designed proportional faces: their outlines sit on a pixel
grid, so baking with FONTBAKE_EM=1 at a grid-aligned ppem gives an atlas
with no partial coverage at all (fontbake reports "gray 0.0%"). Grid
sizes measured: Mini and Blocks are exact at em 8/16/24/32, High at
16/32; Pixel is within ~3% at 16/32. Off-grid sizes (e.g. em 12, 20)
reintroduce antialiasing and look lumpy when thresholded.
